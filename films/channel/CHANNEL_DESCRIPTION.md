# Quietly Human · channel description

Paste into YouTube Studio → Customization → Basic info → Description.

---

Short animated films about the things we don't say out loud.

Saying yes to everyone. Saying "I'm fine" when you're not. Betting to win it back. Carrying a grudge, running on 1%, putting off the call to your dad.

No narrator. Just a story, a few words on screen, and the voice in your head that tells you "later."

New film every week. If one of them is about you, you're not the only one.

---

Notes
- The first line is what shows under the channel name in search and on the channel page, so it carries the promise on its own (Day One's does the same: "Animated short films about discipline, struggle, ambition, and becoming better.").
- The second paragraph names the films' topics in plain words, which helps search.
- "New film every week" sets a schedule; change it if the schedule changes.
- Channel keywords (Studio → Settings → Channel → Basic info): animated short film, 2d animation, short film, mental health, people pleasing, burnout, betting addiction, i'm fine, self improvement, life lessons
